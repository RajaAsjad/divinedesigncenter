About Us

Design first.
Products second.

For over two decades, Divine Design Center has been Boston's destination for luxury whole-home design. We merge European craftsmanship with American livability — creating interiors that feel both timeless and deeply personal.

Mariette & Magued Barsoum founded Divine in 2004. Twenty years of trusted design, thousands of completed projects, and a team of dedicated interior designers and architects.

Meet Samantha, Gaya, Ariana, and our full in-house design team — from schematic design through construction administration.
